# Biodiversity in National Parks Analysis

## Project Overview
An analysis of species conservation data across four U.S. National Parks (Yellowstone, Yosemite, Bryce Canyon, and Great Smoky Mountains) to investigate taxonomic vulnerability, federal protection rates, and geographic observation frequency.

## Key Findings
- **Protection Rates:** Mammals (17.05%) and Birds (15.37%) exhibit the highest proportion under federal protection, while Vascular Plants represent over 4,000 species but maintain an intervention rate of ~1.08%.
- **Statistical Significance:** A Chi-Square ($\chi^2$) test showed no statistically significant difference between Mammals and Birds ($p \approx 0.6876$), but a statistically significant difference between Mammals and Reptiles ($p \approx 0.0384$).
- **Park Observations:** Yellowstone recorded the highest volume of protected species sightings (>44,500 over a 7-day period).
